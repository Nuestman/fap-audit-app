The central question:
“If a serious injury or acute medical emergency occurs anywhere on this mine site today, can this system recognize it, stabilize the patient, evacuate them safely and rapidly, and deliver them to the right level of definitive care?”

Purpose
This tool is designed to assess whether the mine first-aid post and ambulance service can recognize emergencies, provide safe initial care, stabilize patients, communicate effectively, and transfer patients to an appropriate level of definitive care. It also assesses occupational health activities, resources, clinical governance, documentation, infection prevention, and quality improvement.
How to use the tool
For every audit item, mark Yes, No or N/A; record objective evidence; document the finding; assign a risk rating; and agree a corrective action, responsible person and target completion date. Auditors should verify evidence physically or from records rather than relying only on verbal assurance.
Audit Finding	Meaning	Action Required
Major Non-Conformity (Major NC)	A significant failure to meet one or more requirements of ISO 45001:2018 or a systemic breakdown of the management system that seriously affects its ability to achieve intended results, or where there is a high risk to workers' health and safety.	Immediate corrective action is required and may require follow-up verification.
Minor Non-Conformity (Minor NC)	An isolated failure to meet a requirement of ISO 45001:2018 or the organization's management system that does not indicate a systemic breakdown or significantly affect the achievement of intended outcomes.	Corrective action is required.
Opportunity for Improvement (OFI)	The requirement is met, but there is potential to improve effectiveness, efficiency, or maturity of the management system. This is not a non-conformity.	Improvement is recommended but not mandatory.
Observation (OBS)	A condition or trend that currently complies with ISO 45001 requirements but, if left unaddressed, could develop into a non-conformity. It serves as an early warning.	Monitoring or preventive action is recommended.
Conformance (C)	Objective evidence demonstrates that the requirement of ISO 45001:2018 and the organization's documented management system has been fully implemented and is effective.	No action required.
